About Us

More Than
Just a Bar

Argos is the kind of place you come for a quick drink and end up staying all night. A neighbourhood bar built around real food, proper drinks, and a crowd that knows how to have a good time.

From jalapeño poppers and sticky ribs to wood-fired pizzas and pasta that speaks for itself, we've built a menu worth lingering over. No pretension. Just good stuff.
