# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People looking for a night out or somewhere new to go: groups of friends, couples, local regulars, people browsing for a venue on their phone. They arrive curious and need to feel the energy of the place immediately. The job is converting a browser into a visitor.

## Product Purpose

Argos Leisure Bar's marketing website. Its single job is making new customers want to walk through the door. Success is a visitor who feels the vibe, checks the menu, and either books or shows up.

## Brand Personality

Playful, social, electric. The bar that buzzes. Banter, music, spontaneous great nights. Unapologetically fun without being try-hard or chaotic.

## Anti-references

Cheap and cluttered: low-budget, too much copy, messy layout, dated design. This should feel considered and energetic, not like a flyer printed at home.

Also avoid: generic dark-background + neon-glow bar-chain template sites. No stock-photo-of-cocktail-with-light-bokeh aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. **Feel it before you read it.** The atmosphere should hit on scroll-in; no explanatory intros needed.
2. **Social proof through energy, not copy.** Show the place as it is: busy, warm, buzzing. Let visuals do the persuading.
3. **Direct and honest.** Menu prices visible, hours clear, location easy to find. No dark patterns, no friction.
4. **Mobile first.** Most visitors are on their phone deciding where to go tonight. Every tap target matters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Sufficient contrast on all text. Reduced motion support for animations.
